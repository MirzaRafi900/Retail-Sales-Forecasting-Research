# Research Insights

## Research Insight 01
Sales Distribution

Retail sales are not normally distributed.
Sales values are positively skewed, indicating
the presence of sales spikes and high-demand periods.

---

## Research Insight 02
Store Type Impact

Store Type B consistently generated higher sales
compared to the remaining store categories.

This suggests that store characteristics influence
overall sales performance.

---

## Research Insight 03
Promotion Impact

Promotional activities showed a substantial positive
effect on sales performance.

Stores operating promotional campaigns generated
significantly higher average and median sales values
than stores without active promotions.

---

## Research Insight 04
Day of Week Effect

Sales vary across different days of the week.

Day 1 recorded the highest average sales levels,
while Day 7 recorded substantially lower sales activity,
suggesting operational or customer-behavior patterns.

---

## Research Insight 05
Store Availability

Store operational status directly impacts sales.

Closed stores generated no sales, while open stores
recorded an average sales value of approximately 6,956.

---

## Research Insight 06
Customer Influence

Customer count demonstrated the strongest relationship
with sales performance.

Correlation analysis produced a coefficient of 0.89
between Customers and Sales, indicating a very strong
positive association.

As customer traffic increases, sales revenue generally
increases as well.

This variable is expected to be one of the most
important predictors within future forecasting models.

---

## Research Insight 07
Feature Importance Candidates

Based on exploratory analysis, the following variables
are likely to play important roles in forecasting:

- Customers
- Open
- Promo
- DayOfWeek
- StoreType

These variables will be prioritized during feature
engineering and machine learning model development.
