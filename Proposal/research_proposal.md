# Research Proposal

## Title

A Comparative Study of Machine Learning Models
for Retail Sales Forecasting

## Background

Retail businesses require accurate sales forecasts
for inventory management and decision-making.

## Problem Statement

Selecting the most appropriate machine learning
model for retail sales forecasting remains challenging.

## Aim

To compare machine learning algorithms for retail
sales forecasting.

## Objectives

1. Compare forecasting models.
2. Evaluate forecasting performance.
3. Identify strengths and limitations.
4. Recommend suitable models.

## Research Questions

RQ1:
Which model performs best?

RQ2:
How do models compare in forecasting accuracy?

## Expected Contribution

Provide insights into machine learning model
selection for retail forecasting applications.
